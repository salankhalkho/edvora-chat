The Strategic Guide to Knowledge Management in Edvora Chat

Powering Intent-Grounded, 100% Accurate AI Conversations with Zero Outdated Information • Click to read full guide

Read Guide
T
The Core Mission: The Knowledge Hub (#knowledge) is not just a document warehouse; it is the active cognitive boundary for your institution’s chatbot. A prospective student asking about admissions, fee structures, hostels, or scholarships expects immediate, exact, and contextually satisfying answers. However, giving an outdated 2024 fee figure or hallucinating a deadline can cost the university student trust and enrollment. This compendium details how to leverage every function on the Knowledge Hub to ensure your chatbot accurately identifies student intent, strictly isolates information between departments, and instantly silences outdated policies without needing AI retraining.
Step-by-Step Operations: How to Get the Best Out of Each Function
1

Ingesting Grounding Material

Your chatbot answers solely based on indexed institutional knowledge via the + Add or Upload Document action.
  • PDF / Circular Upload: Official prospectus brochures, scholarship policies, hostel rulebooks, or placement statistics.
  • Direct Web URL Sync: Dynamic web portals (e.g., admissions.apex.edu/dates) synced on a recurring schedule.
  • Direct Text Paste: Unstructured FAQs or rapid circulars (e.g., "Helpline numbers during counseling week").
Intent Rule: Use clean numerical tables and specific headings to allow the AI to parse direct answers for cutoff and fee queries without hesitation.
2

Departmental Scoping & Silo Isolation

Tagging every document to its specific campus department (e.g., Undergraduate Admissions, Bursar & Financial Aid, Student Affairs, Career Cell, Compliance).
  • Zero Cross-Talk: When a candidate in the MBA stream asks about tuition, the bot pulls exclusively from the MBA Admissions & Bursar scope rather than confusing them with undergraduate hostel deposit guidelines.
  • Global Scope Toggle: Institutional circulars (anti-ragging, campus address, accreditations) can be marked global for cross-bot availability.
3

Freshness Shield: Temporal Guardrails

Outdated information is the #1 reason users lose trust in AI chatbots. The Freshness Shield prevents this through automatic lifecycle dates (effective_from & expires_on):
  • Permanent / Evergreen Policy: Perennial rules (e.g., Campus Code of Conduct, Mission Statement).
  • Academic Cycle Validity: Tied directly to an academic year (e.g., Aug 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027).
  • Auto-Quarantine on Expiry: When expires_on arrives, the system instantly excludes the document from the AI's search context at the database query level. The bot immediately stops citing old fees or deadlines—no manual deletion or retraining needed.
4

Review Cadence & Dean Audit Sign-Off

Setting proactive review intervals (e.g., Every 6 Months, Annual Review) to ensure ongoing accuracy.
  • Expiring Soon (≤ 30 Days Left): Amber warning alerts administrators that an admission or scholarship policy cycle is concluding.
  • Review Due: Triggers an audit requirement for the respective department head to review and sign off before the new intake commences.
5

Document Management Studio (✏️ Manage)

Clicking any document title or ✏️ Manage opens the document editor studio:
  • Inspect Processed Chunks: See exactly how the retrieval engine parsed and extracted text chunks for grounding.
  • Adjust Keywords & Intent Matchers: Add targeted synonyms (e.g., adding tuition waiver, fee discount, merit grant to the Scholarship document ensures high retrieval relevance).
  • 1-Click Academic Rollover: Seamlessly replace an outdated prospectus with the new year's edition. Predecessors are archived with audit trails while fresh docs take over live traffic.
6

Search, Filter & Pagination Controls

Efficiently govern large institutional repositories across cycles:
  • Instant Search (⌘K): Type any keyword, academic version, or title to instantly find specific documentation.
  • Lifecycle Status Tabs: Filter by Active & Live, Needs Attention, Expiring Soon, or Archived History.
  • Custom Pagination: Switch between 10 (Default), 25, 50, 100, or 250 rows per page to review large repositories efficiently.
Best Practices: The 5 Golden Rules for Precision Chatbot Answers
# Rule What to Do in Knowledge Hub Expected Chatbot Behavior
1 No Duplicate Versions When uploading a new version of an existing document, use 1-Click Rollover / Replace instead of adding a second parallel PDF. The chatbot never gets confused between two different years' fee schedules.
2 Rich Keywords for Intent In the document editor, ensure important search terms (e.g. hostel, mess fees, room sharing, laundry) are populated in the keyword matrix. The AI accurately maps conversational slang (e.g. "What do I pay for staying on campus?") to the exact fee table.
3 Enforce Expiry Dates on Fees Always set expires_on to the end of the academic year for anything involving monetary amounts, dates, or entrance exams. Eliminates the risk of quoting expired discount slabs or invalid exam cutoffs.
4 Scope to Correct Department Always assign program-specific policies to their dedicated Department (e.g., School of Engineering). Preserves student context and provides tailored answers for the specific degree program.
5 Check "Needs Attention" Weekly Click the Needs Attention tab on the toolbar once a week to address upcoming expiries or review-due documents. Guarantees zero downtime and 100% continuous grounded knowledge.
Summary: By applying these practices within the Knowledge Hub, your AI chatbot operates as a reliable, fully grounded 24/7 institutional counselor—answering student inquiries with pinpoint accuracy, understanding diverse question intents, and remaining permanently insulated against hallucinated or outdated information.
