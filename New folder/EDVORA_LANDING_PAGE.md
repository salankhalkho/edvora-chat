# Edvora.chat — Landing Page Content & Design Direction

**Status:** Ready for implementation
**Primary audience:** College & University decision-makers
**Primary buyers:** Admissions Directors, Enrollment/Marketing leaders, Digital leaders, University leadership
**Primary objective:** Generate qualified demo requests
**Secondary objective:** Clearly establish Edvora as an **AI admissions conversion engine**, not merely a chatbot.

---

# 1. Core Positioning

## Category

> **AI Admissions Conversion Engine**

## One-line description

> **Edvora turns student conversations into admissions opportunities.**

## Primary promise

> **Turn More Website Visitors Into Qualified Students.**

## Supporting message

> Edvora turns your college website into a 24/7 admissions engine—answering questions, capturing high-intent leads, qualifying prospects, routing conversations to the right team, and helping move students closer to enrollment.

### Secondary

* Admissions counselors

* International admissions teams

---

# 4. What We Are Selling

We are **not primarily selling AI**.

We are selling:

### More opportunities captured

Visitors who would otherwise leave can become identifiable prospects.

### Better-qualified leads

Counselors receive more context before contacting a prospect.

### Faster response

Students can receive answers and request human assistance immediately.

### Better counselor productivity

Counselors spend more time on valuable conversations and less time answering repetitive questions.

### Better admissions intelligence

Institutions learn what prospective students are actually asking about.

---

# 5. What We Should NOT Say

Avoid making the homepage primarily about:

> "Our advanced AI technology."

Avoid:

> "Revolutionary conversational AI."

Avoid:

> "Next-generation LLM-powered university chatbot."

Avoid technical explanations such as:

> "Our proprietary AI orchestration architecture..."

Technology can appear lower on the page.

The buyer cares first about:

> **"Will this help us convert more prospective students?"**

# 14. FOUR CORE CAPABILITIES

## Capture

### Capture interest while it's high.

> Edvora can naturally ask for contact information when a student shows meaningful intent—such as asking about fees, requesting a prospectus or exploring scholarships.

---

## Qualify

### Know more than just a name and phone number.

> Capture relevant admissions context through conversation, including program interest, academic information, location, entrance-exam information and enrollment intent.

The product architecture supports program interest, entrance exam, scores, graduation, work experience and admission intent as qualification data. 

---

## Route

### Get every inquiry to the right person.

> Route conversations and leads based on department, program, availability, intent and other configured rules.

The underlying system supports department-based, program-based, availability-based, capacity-based and other counselor-assignment models. 

---

## Convert

### Turn conversations into action.

> Help students take the next step—request a callback, book an appointment, schedule a campus visit, explore scholarships, download resources or begin an application.

* Accordions
* Tabs

* Scroll effects

* Simple interactions

# 50. PROOF / METRIC POLICY

The existing Edvora source documents contain numerous potential marketing claims, including:

* `3.8x` verified lead volume
* `3.4x` lead completion
* `65%` lower CPQL
* `95+` languages
* `98%` WhatsApp open rates
* `3.5x` campus attendance
* `90%` automated routine queries
* `99.4%` document-grounded accuracy

These should **not automatically be presented as established Edvora customer results**.

### Rule

Only publish a numerical performance claim when it has:

1. A defined methodology
2. A reliable source
3. A real customer/product measurement
4. Approval for public marketing use

Until then, use:

> **Illustrative**

or remove the number.

This is particularly important for an enterprise higher-education audience.
